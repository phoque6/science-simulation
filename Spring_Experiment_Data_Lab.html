Teacher and student guide

Use the same spring and measurement method for every load. Read the ruler at eye level and follow your teacher’s safe loading limit. The “Add next mass” button suggests a 50 g step; it does not set a safe maximum.

The original length is your recorded unloaded reference, not a three-trial mean. The app uses that same original length for every extension calculation. Raw readings are preserved. A negative extension is shown and flagged for checking, never silently changed or removed.

Changing the original length recalculates all extensions. Incomplete or invalid rows have no mean. Duplicate masses are flagged and excluded until corrected. Keep unusual readings, discuss them and remeasure if needed; the app does not automatically remove outliers.

The app works offline. Work is saved in this browser when available; use Save work for a portable backup, including reflections. CSV includes raw readings, calculations and reflections. Nothing is automatically sent to your teacher or an AI service. Calculations are mathematical, not live AI feedback.
