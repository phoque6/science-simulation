Experiment guide

Read the bottom of the spring at eye level. The ruler’s zero is aligned with the top of the spring. Find the final length, then subtract the original length.

Extension = final length − original length.

For a fair test, change only the added mass. Keep the same spring, starting length and measurement method.

This idealised spring starts at 10 cm and extends 1 cm per 50 g over 0–200 g. The model ignores hanger mass and measurement error. It does not represent every real spring or predict behaviour beyond this range. The brief settling motion is illustrative.
